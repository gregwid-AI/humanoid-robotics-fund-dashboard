What this fund is

Humanoid Robotics Actuator Seed Fund · 2026 target vintage

Cross-platform hardware strategy

This strategy invests in the specialized hardware bottlenecks required to scale humanoid robots—linear and rotary actuators, precision reducers, servo drives, and related motion-control components—rather than taking concentrated exposure to a single robot OEM.

The target basket combines established Tier-1 suppliers across China, Korea, Japan, and the United States. The thesis is that suppliers can benefit as multiple humanoid platforms move from pilot lines to commercial production, while their existing automotive, industrial, or defense businesses provide a downside backstop.

Core thesisMonetize the factory bottleneck: enter during pilot yield constraints, hold through production ramp, harvest as volume reaches scale, and exit before actuator ASPs normalize toward automotive economics.
